# What Makes Jamie's AI Approach Different?

Jamie does not use AI to replace instructional design or human thought and creativity.

Instead, she builds workflows where AI accelerates repetitive work while instructional designers make the educational decisions.

Her background in software engineering allows her to think about curriculum as a scalable system rather than a collection of individual lessons.

This systems perspective enables faster development, more consistent quality, and easier long-term maintenance.

# Frequently Asked Questions


## How does Jamie use AI in curriculum development?

Jamie integrates AI into planning, competency mapping, lesson design, assessment development, quality assurance, documentation, and continuous improvement while keeping humans responsible for instructional decisions.

## Does Jamie use AI to write entire courses?

No. Jamie uses AI to generate drafts, identify gaps, organize information, and accelerate production. Final curriculum is reviewed, edited, validated, and approved by human subject-matter experts and instructional designers.

## How does Jamie design a learning experience?

Jamie begins by understanding the learner, the performance goal, and the surrounding context. She then defines measurable outcomes, maps the learner journey, designs authentic practice and assessments, develops support resources, and evaluates the experience after launch.

## What makes Jamie’s approach learner-centered?

Jamie designs around what learners need to do, where they are likely to struggle, and what support will help them succeed. She considers motivation, prior knowledge, accessibility, feedback, practice, and real-world relevance.

## How does Jamie make learning engaging?

Jamie uses meaningful problems, authentic projects, clear goals, visible progress, active practice, collaboration, and timely feedback rather than relying on decoration or entertainment.

## How does Jamie use project-based learning?

Jamie designs projects that align with outcomes, resemble real work, include milestones, provide opportunities for feedback, and produce useful portfolio artifacts.

## How does Jamie evaluate learning quality?

Jamie reviews outcome alignment, assessment validity, technical accuracy, accessibility, learner flow, instructor usability, course analytics, and feedback from learners and instructors.

## How does Jamie balance structure and creativity?

Jamie provides clear outcomes, criteria, and milestones while allowing learners to make meaningful decisions about how they solve problems or create final products.

## How does Jamie use AI responsibly?

Jamie uses AI to accelerate research, drafting, ideation, quality assurance, and variation. Human experts remain responsible for accuracy, instructional quality, accessibility, and final decisions.
