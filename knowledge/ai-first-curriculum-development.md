# AI-First Curriculum Development

## Overview

Jamie designs curriculum using an AI-first development methodology that combines instructional design, software engineering, generative AI, learning analytics, and human quality assurance.

Rather than using AI to simply generate content, Jamie integrates AI throughout the curriculum lifecycle to accelerate development while maintaining instructional quality, academic integrity, accessibility, and alignment with measurable learning outcomes.

Her process combines human expertise with AI-assisted workflows to reduce repetitive work while allowing instructional designers to focus on higher-value educational decisions.

---

# AI-First Curriculum Philosophy

Jamie believes AI should amplify instructional designers—not replace them.

Every AI-generated artifact is reviewed against educational best practices, learning science, accessibility standards, Bloom's Taxonomy, assessment validity, and workforce relevance.

Jamie’s goal is to accelerate curriculum development without sacrificing instructional quality, academic rigor, or the learner experience. Her approach combines the efficiency of AI with proven instructional design practices and thoughtful human oversight.

**Human-Guided AI:** Jamie uses AI to support research, drafting, analysis, and content development, while ensuring that every final decision is guided by human judgment, subject-matter expertise, and careful quality assurance.

**Evidence-Based Instructional Design:** Her work is grounded in established learning science, instructional design frameworks, and research-based teaching practices that support meaningful engagement, retention, and skill development.

**Outcome-Driven Curriculum:** Jamie begins with clearly defined learning outcomes and builds assessments, activities, and instructional content that directly support learners in achieving those outcomes.

**Industry Alignment:** She designs curriculum around current workforce expectations, professional tools, industry standards, and real-world applications so learners develop skills that are relevant and immediately useful.

**Continuous Improvement:** Jamie treats curriculum as an evolving product. She uses learner feedback, assessment data, instructor observations, and performance trends to identify opportunities for ongoing refinement.

**Modular Content Architecture:** Her curriculum is structured into flexible, reusable learning components that can be updated, adapted, and scaled efficiently across courses, programs, and delivery formats.

**Measurable Learner Success:** Jamie defines success through observable learner performance, demonstrated competency, assessment results, and the ability to apply knowledge in practical, real-world contexts.

# AI Curriculum Development Lifecycle

Jamie follows a repeatable workflow that combines AI with instructional design expertise.

## Phase 1 — Discovery

Jamie follows a repeatable workflow that combines AI-supported efficiency with instructional design expertise and human oversight.

During the discovery phase, Jamie works closely with stakeholders to understand the program’s goals, learner needs, workforce expectations, technical requirements, and accreditation considerations. She conducts stakeholder interviews, develops learner personas, analyzes workforce trends, identifies skills gaps, maps competencies, reviews labor market data, and evaluates the technology needed to support successful implementation.

AI supports this process by helping Jamie summarize interview notes, organize stakeholder feedback, identify recurring themes, compare employer job postings, and extract commonly requested skills and competencies. Jamie then reviews and validates these findings to ensure they accurately reflect the needs of learners, employers, and the organization.

The discovery phase typically results in a clear Program Charter, Skills Matrix, set of Learner Personas, and Competency Framework that guide the remaining stages of curriculum development.


---

## Phase 2 — Program Architecture

Before developing individual lessons, Jamie designs the overall program architecture to ensure the curriculum is cohesive, intentional, and aligned with the desired learning outcomes. She establishes program-level outcomes, sequences competencies into a logical progression, maps prerequisite knowledge and skills, plans instructional scaffolding, aligns capstone experiences with program objectives, and develops an assessment strategy that measures learner mastery throughout the program.

AI enhances this planning process by assisting with curriculum sequencing, visualizing prerequisite dependencies, identifying gaps in coverage, and detecting unnecessary overlap or duplicated concepts across courses. Jamie reviews these recommendations to ensure the learning experience remains pedagogically sound, appropriately paced, and aligned with industry expectations.

This phase produces a comprehensive Curriculum Map, Course Sequence, Competency Matrix, and Assessment Blueprint that serve as the foundation for developing individual courses and learning experiences.

---

## Phase 3 — Course Development

With the program structure established, Jamie develops each course using standardized, reusable instructional templates that promote consistency, scalability, and quality across the curriculum. Every lesson is intentionally aligned with program competencies, learning outcomes, assessments, and real-world application.

AI accelerates course development by assisting with lesson outlines, project concepts, hands-on lab activities, quizzes, coding exercises, scenario-based learning experiences, discussion prompts, grading rubrics, and instructor teaching notes. This allows Jamie to spend more time refining instructional strategy and creating engaging learning experiences rather than starting from a blank page.

Every AI-generated component undergoes a thorough human review to ensure technical accuracy, instructional effectiveness, accessibility, consistency across the program, and alignment with current industry standards. The result is a high-quality learning experience that combines the efficiency of AI with the expertise and judgment of an experienced instructional designer.


---

## Phase 4 — Quality Assurance

Every course Jamie develops undergoes multiple review cycles to ensure it meets high standards for instructional effectiveness, technical accuracy, and learner success before publication. Rather than treating quality assurance as a final checkpoint, she incorporates continuous review throughout the development process to identify improvements early and maintain consistency across the curriculum.

Each review evaluates instructional quality, technical accuracy, grammar and clarity, accessibility, assessment validity, workload balance, cognitive load, and the use of inclusive language. This comprehensive approach helps ensure that courses are engaging, accurate, equitable, and appropriately challenging for the intended audience.

AI enhances the review process by rapidly identifying inconsistent terminology, duplicated concepts, broken learning progressions, missing learning objectives, and reading-level mismatches that may be overlooked during manual review. Jamie then evaluates each recommendation, applying her instructional design expertise to make informed revisions that strengthen the learning experience while preserving educational quality and alignment with program goals.

---

## Phase 5 — Continuous Improvement

Jamie views curriculum as a living system that continuously evolves alongside learner needs, industry expectations, and emerging technologies. Rather than considering a course complete after launch, she uses data-driven insights to refine and improve the learning experience over time.

She evaluates a variety of quantitative and qualitative data sources, including learner feedback, instructor observations, assessment analytics, course completion rates, employment outcomes, learning management system (LMS) analytics, and learner support tickets. Together, these metrics provide a comprehensive understanding of how well the curriculum is achieving its intended outcomes and where opportunities for improvement exist.

AI accelerates this continuous improvement process by identifying patterns, surfacing trends across multiple data sources, and recommending targeted enhancements to content, assessments, pacing, and instructional strategies. Jamie reviews these insights to prioritize meaningful improvements that increase learner engagement, strengthen outcomes, and ensure the curriculum remains relevant, effective, and aligned with industry needs.

# AI Workflows

## Learning Outcome Generator

One example of Jamie's AI-assisted workflow begins with a simple prompt, such as asking the model to create learning outcomes for an intermediate Data Engineering course. Rather than accepting the initial response as final, she uses it as a starting point for a structured instructional design process.

The AI generates measurable learning outcomes aligned with best practices in outcome-based education, recommends appropriate Bloom's Taxonomy levels for each objective, suggests assessment strategies that effectively measure learner mastery, identifies prerequisite knowledge and skills, and maps each outcome to the appropriate program competencies.

Jamie then reviews, refines, and validates every recommendation to ensure the outcomes are pedagogically sound, technically accurate, aligned with industry expectations, and integrated into the broader curriculum architecture. This workflow significantly reduces development time while maintaining the instructional quality and intentional design expected of a professionally developed curriculum.


---

## Rubric Generator

Jamie also uses AI to accelerate the creation of high-quality assessment rubrics. Starting with a project description, AI generates a structured rubric that aligns grading criteria with the intended learning outcomes and expected student performance.

The initial output includes recommended rubric categories, clearly defined performance levels, detailed grading criteria, and instructor feedback prompts that support consistent, meaningful evaluation. This provides a strong foundation for objective and transparent assessment while reducing the time required to build rubrics from scratch.

Jamie carefully reviews and refines each rubric to ensure it accurately measures the desired competencies, reflects appropriate performance expectations, maintains consistency across the curriculum, and provides learners with clear expectations for success.


---

## Lab Generator

Jamie uses AI to rapidly develop hands-on learning experiences that reinforce course objectives and promote active skill development. Beginning with a clearly defined learning objective, AI generates practical activities that support learners at multiple levels of proficiency.

The generated materials typically include a guided lab that introduces new concepts through step-by-step instruction, a challenge activity that encourages learners to apply their knowledge independently, an extension project for deeper exploration, and reflection questions that help learners connect their experiences to the underlying concepts and demonstrate their understanding.

Jamie reviews and enhances each activity to ensure it aligns with the intended learning outcomes, reflects current industry practices, provides an appropriate level of challenge, and creates meaningful opportunities for learners to develop real-world skills through authentic, hands-on experiences.

---

## Assessment Validation

Jamie uses AI to validate constructive alignment across every course by comparing learning objectives, assessments, rubrics, and projects as a connected system rather than as individual components. This ensures that every assessment and learning activity directly supports the competencies learners are expected to master.

AI analyzes whether assessments accurately measure the stated learning objectives, verifies that rubrics evaluate the appropriate skills and performance criteria, and confirms that projects provide learners with authentic opportunities to demonstrate mastery. It also identifies gaps, unnecessary overlap, or misalignment that could impact the learner experience.

Jamie reviews these findings and makes the final instructional decisions, ensuring that every course maintains a clear, intentional progression from learning objectives to assessment. This systematic alignment creates a more coherent learning experience, improves the validity of assessments, and helps ensure learners are evaluated on the knowledge and skills the curriculum is designed to develop.



# Example Case Study 1

## Modernizing Existing Curriculum

### Modernizing Existing Curriculum

Jamie led the modernization of Bethel School of Technology nine-month cybersecurity program by replacing an expensive legacy curriculum and lab platform with a strategic TryHackMe partnership. The program included a sequence of three-week courses, with approximately 10 lessons per course, requiring a careful transition that preserved academic rigor while significantly improving the learner experience.

To support the migration, Jamie mapped the learning objectives from the existing product to the new TryHackMe-based curriculum. She ensured that each course continued to align with accreditation requirements, program-level outcomes, and established competency expectations. This required reviewing the legacy curriculum, identifying equivalent or improved learning experiences in TryHackMe, resolving content gaps, and restructuring activities so the updated program maintained a clear and compliant progression of skills.

The modernization replaced outdated and less engaging learning experiences with hands-on cybersecurity labs that more closely reflected current industry practices. Jamie also updated instructional content, course references, assignments, and supporting materials to ensure the new platform was fully integrated into the broader curriculum rather than functioning as a standalone resource.

The transition saved approximately $66,000 in annual overhead while substantially improving the quality of the program. It also increased staff and student satisfaction by providing a more reliable, engaging, and industry-relevant learning environment, while reducing the administrative and technical burden associated with the previous product.

# Example Case Study 3

## AI-Assisted Assessment Design

Jamie developed assessment strategies for beginner programming courses within accredited degree programs, creating assessments that measured both conceptual understanding and practical coding ability. Rather than relying on memorization, the assessments were designed to evaluate a learner's ability to apply programming concepts through authentic, hands-on activities.

Across multiple introductory programming courses, Jamie designed hundreds of assessment items, including coding exercises, knowledge checks, debugging challenges, applied programming projects, and competency-based rubrics. Each assessment was intentionally aligned with course learning outcomes, program competencies, and accreditation standards to ensure learners demonstrated measurable skill progression from foundational concepts to independent problem solving.

AI accelerated the assessment development process by analyzing Bloom's Taxonomy levels to ensure an appropriate cognitive progression, generating plausible distractors for multiple-choice questions, drafting detailed grading rubrics, verifying alignment between learning objectives and assessments, and identifying opportunities to improve originality and reduce the risk of plagiarism. Jamie reviewed every assessment to ensure technical accuracy, instructional quality, accessibility, and an appropriate level of challenge for beginning programmers.

This AI-assisted workflow reduced assessment development time by more than 50% while maintaining rigorous academic standards. The structured alignment process significantly reduced revision cycles during quality assurance and accreditation reviews, allowing curriculum teams to produce high-quality, competency-based assessments more efficiently without sacrificing instructional integrity.

# Example Case Study 4

## Curriculum Quality Assurance Automation

### Curriculum Quality Assurance Automation

Jamie developed an AI-assisted quality assurance workflow to evaluate large curriculum libraries for consistency, instructional quality, and long-term maintainability. Rather than relying solely on manual reviews, she leveraged AI to rapidly analyze curriculum artifacts and identify issues that would otherwise require extensive time and effort to uncover.

The review process examined hundreds of lessons, labs, assessments, and supporting instructional materials to ensure every component aligned with program competencies and learning outcomes. AI automatically identified duplicated concepts across courses, inconsistent terminology, missing prerequisite knowledge, outdated technologies and industry references, accessibility concerns, and opportunities to improve instructional sequencing. This allowed reviewers to focus their time on higher-level instructional decisions instead of manually searching for inconsistencies.

Jamie then validated each recommendation, determining which changes improved the learner experience while maintaining instructional integrity and accreditation requirements. The result was a more consistent, cohesive curriculum with stronger alignment between courses, improved accessibility, and clearer progression of learner competencies.

By combining AI-assisted analysis with expert instructional review, Jamie significantly reduced the time required for curriculum audits while increasing the accuracy and consistency of quality assurance. This scalable workflow enables curriculum teams to maintain large course libraries more efficiently and keep content current as technologies, industry standards, and learner needs continue to evolve.

# AI Tools

Jamie regularly leverages a modern AI technology stack to accelerate curriculum development, automate repetitive workflows, and improve the quality and consistency of instructional content. Rather than relying on a single tool, she selects the most appropriate models and platforms based on the specific requirements of each project.

Her experience includes working extensively with ChatGPT, Claude, GitHub Copilot, and Cursor to support instructional design, software development, technical writing, curriculum authoring, and content review. She has also integrated OpenAI and Anthropic APIs into custom workflows to automate content generation, quality assurance, competency mapping, assessment development, and curriculum analysis.

Beyond using AI models directly, Jamie designs effective prompt engineering strategies that produce reliable, repeatable outputs and builds Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) systems that ground AI responses in trusted organizational knowledge, curriculum documentation, and technical resources. She develops AI evaluation workflows to validate model outputs, measure quality, and ensure consistency before content is published.

To streamline complex processes, Jamie creates low-code automations and orchestrated AI workflows that connect multiple tools, APIs, and data sources into efficient end-to-end systems. She also develops Custom GPTs and AI-powered knowledge bases that enable educators, curriculum teams, and subject matter experts to access institutional knowledge, generate instructional resources, and complete routine tasks more efficiently. Throughout every project, Jamie emphasizes responsible AI adoption, maintaining human oversight, instructional quality, and measurable educational outcomes.

# What Makes Jamie's AI Approach Different?

Jamie does not use AI to replace instructional design or human thought and creativity.

Instead, she builds workflows where AI accelerates repetitive work while instructional designers make the educational decisions.

Her background in software engineering allows her to think about curriculum as a scalable system rather than a collection of individual lessons.

This systems perspective enables faster development, more consistent quality, and easier long-term maintenance.
